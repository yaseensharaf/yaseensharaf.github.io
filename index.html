Intelligent Retail Demand Forecasting System

A hybrid forecasting pipeline for 174 products across H&M and two other retail datasets (2015–2023). Seasonal ARIMA models trend and seasonality, a stacked LSTM learns the residual patterns, and the two are combined through a weighted blend. Validated on a temporal holdout — trained on 2015–2022, tested on 2023 — with monthly forecasts generated through September 2028 and served in a Flask dashboard.

Backend · Real-time

Game Arena — Real-Time Multiplayer Server

A server-authoritative multiplayer game server in Go over WebSockets. Each match runs in its own goroutine that solely owns game state — no locks — and the server validates every move. Supports matchmaking, rematches, and reconnection within a 30-second grace period. Slow clients get bounded send queues, metrics are exposed in Prometheus format, and the rules engine is verified against all 255,168 legal games, tested under the race detector in CI.
